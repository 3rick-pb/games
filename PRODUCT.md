# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js with TypeScript, React Server Components by default, native CSS design tokens, and Vercel deployment. This minimizes client JavaScript while keeping feature routes modular and deployment straightforward.

## Users

Spanish-speaking people who need to complete everyday productivity, calculation, conversion, personal-finance, and lightweight digital tasks quickly from a phone or desktop, without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy, fast workspace for useful standalone tools. Success means a visitor can find the appropriate tool, understand its result, and complete the task without unnecessary data collection or friction.

## Positioning

A privacy-first, Spanish-first utility suite that combines the discoverability and polish of a product workspace with tools that operate locally whenever possible.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from direct use and search. They may use a small phone, tablet, laptop, or large display; keyboard access and a clear reading path are required. Basic tools work without accounts and without a database.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Initial areas: productivity, mathematics, conversions, personal finance, time, generators, and technology.
- Initial tools include Pomodoro, time calculations, counters, unit conversion, QR generation, secure passwords, mathematical calculators, and educational finance calculators.
- Calculations require validated inputs, human-readable error states, useful explanations, and tests for critical formulas.
- Passwords, QR content, timers, conversions, and ordinary calculations must stay client-side when no server is needed.
- Spanish is the launch locale; the content architecture must allow English later.
- Advertising, analytics, accounts, and a database are future-capable integration points, not launch requirements.

## Evidence on Hand

The product brief is the only supplied product source. No brand assets, logo, screenshots, customer claims, analytics data, legal copy, or domain are currently available. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Utility before traffic, advertising, or novelty.
2. Local-first handling of private inputs.
3. Clear results with enough context to be understood and checked.
4. Fast, accessible interactions across all screen sizes.
5. Modular growth without rebuilding the suite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where reasonable: semantic HTML, visible focus, keyboard operation, appropriate labels, contrast, screen-reader support, and reduced-motion support.
