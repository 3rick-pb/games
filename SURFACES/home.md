# Home surface

## Visitor success

Find the right utility and enter it immediately, with enough context to trust the result.

## Information priority

1. Global search and a small set of useful starting tools.
2. Category navigation and recently added or popular options.
3. Concise educational guidance, related tools, and transparent site information.

## States

Search has default, focused, populated, no-results, and keyboard-navigation states. Advertising slots are absent unless explicitly enabled by configuration.
